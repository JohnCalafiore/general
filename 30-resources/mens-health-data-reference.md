# Men's health data reference — DTD's citable numbers

The evidence base DTD uses to make its case, assembled Aug 19 2026 and sent to Andrew for
review as `DTD_Mens_Health_Data_Reference.md`. Built for Frankie Spontelli's Instagram reel
but useful anywhere DTD needs numbers: grant applications, the Man Therapy blog post, the
board pitch deck, funder conversations.

John's framing of the standard: "**Everything in it is sourced to CDC, peer-reviewed
journals, or named national surveys, with the data year on each figure so nothing goes
stale on you.**"

## The headline figures

- **About 4 out of 5 people who die by suicide in the US are men.**
- The **rural suicide rate is roughly 50% higher than the urban rate** — rural men die at
  about **30 per 100,000** versus **21** for urban men.
- In **Colorado, men are 77% of suicide deaths**, and the state **ranks in the top 10
  nationally**.
- **Chaffee County's rate is close to double the Colorado average** — the county Buena
  Vista sits in.
- In **1990, 3% of men said they had no close friends. Today it's 15%.** The share with six
  or more close friends fell from **55% to 27%**.
- **Two-thirds of young men say "no one really knows me."**
- Loneliness raises the risk of early death about as much as **smoking 15 cigarettes a
  day**; strong social ties raise odds of survival by **50%**.
- Men are about **half as likely as women to receive any mental health treatment**, and
  **70% of rural counties have no psychiatrist at all**.

## The argument the numbers make

John's own synthesis, worth reusing verbatim: "**That last pairing is the whole argument
for what we do.** Men aren't walking into clinics, and out here there often isn't a clinic
to walk into. **A dinner has no intake form, no waitlist, no co-pay, and no record.**"

On the format itself: a systematic review of **Australia's Men's Sheds** found men connect
"**shoulder to shoulder**" rather than face to face — "basically a description of a dinner
table."

## Safe messaging — read before publishing anything on this topic

Section 9 of the attachment carries **AFSP's safe messaging guidelines for creators**. The
essentials:

- Say "**died by suicide**," never "committed suicide."
- **Never reference methods.**
- Focus on **the whole arc**, not the crisis moment.
- Put **988 in the caption and on screen**.

This goes to anyone making DTD content — it was sent to Frankie precisely because he is
telling a personal story about depression.
