# Vision, mission, and tagline — the finalized language

Settled on **2026-09-10** in a 63-minute working session with **Jennifer Ashley** (Vibrant
Emotional Health / JXA Advisors), after the board had deferred the question twice since August.
Use this language verbatim in grant applications, the pitch deck, the website, and anywhere DTD
describes itself. It is **not yet board-ratified** — see the caveat at the end.

## Vision

> **We envision a world where no man has to navigate life alone and is part of a community of
> genuine friendship and belonging.**

## Mission

> **Our mission is to reduce social isolation in men by fostering connection and building
> community.**

## Tagline

> **Connecting men in a disconnected world**

Unchanged, and deliberately so — the session concluded it "will remain the official tagline."

## How each was arrived at

**Vision.** The starting point was the existing bylaw statement, judged "strong but needed
refinement." The specific problem was one phrase: "**the term 'safe place' felt clinical and
created a mental health barrier**." The fix was to replace it with "**community of genuine
friendship and belonging**."

This is the same judgment reached independently in the 2026-09-04 positioning work, which chose
"third space" over "safe space" for the same reason. Two separate sessions landing on the same
objection is worth noting: **"safe space" is the phrase DTD keeps deciding not to use.**

**Mission.** The test applied was that "**the mission must define the core 'why,' not just the
'what' (dinners)**." The tagline was assessed as "powerful but **incomplete as a mission**" — it
names the condition but not the response. The resolution was to state the **problem** (social
isolation) and the **method** (fostering connection, building community), leaving dinners as the
vehicle rather than the definition. The word "dinners" appears nowhere in either statement, which
is what makes them survive a Discord server, a corporate training programme, or whatever comes
next.

## "Third place" as the marketing angle

Confirmed in the same session: **DTD as a "third place" (after home and work)** is "a powerful,
unique marketing angle that resonates with members." This is now the settled frame — chosen over
"safe space" on 2026-09-04 and reaffirmed here.

## What supersedes what

Two earlier working drafts are now **retired** and should not be reused:

- Vision: "A world where no man walks alone"
- Mission: "When men connect, communities get stronger"

Jennifer's earlier counsel — that the vision "should go deeper than '1,000 dinners' — closer to
'every man has a sense of belonging'" (2026-09-03) — is visibly carried into the final wording.

## Caveat: not yet ratified

The Fathom recap's next steps put the ball with all three parties: **Andrew and John are to
"finalize the vision and mission statements internally,"** and Jennifer is to "email Andrew &
John vision/mission/tagline draft; **request review/board feedback**." Her standing process
advice from 2026-09-03 applies — "keep [the board] out of the kitchen on vision/mission; bring
a recommendation" — so the **Sep 18 board meeting** should receive this as a proposal to approve,
not a draft to reopen.

Until that happens, treat this file as the working language rather than adopted policy.
