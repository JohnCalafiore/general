## meet-granola-dpp-20260812 Granola: DPP Roundtable — AI and the Future of Nonprofit Work

Aug 12, 10:00am MDT. Speakers: Gail Roberts, Paul Roach, Remy Reya.

Discussion highlights:
- **Can AI do fundraising?** Remy's framing: AI should augment **head work** (strategy,
  expertise) and automate **hands work** (data entry, rote tasks); **heart work**
  (relationships, empathy) stays human.
- **Where AI is winning:** grant writing (major productivity gains synthesizing complex
  ideas and tailoring to funders); newsletters and routine copy (largely AI-generated
  already); prospect research — tools like **iWave** and low-cost CRM analytics
  (<$100/month) replace the need for a dedicated researcher; network-based relationship
  mapping — **Happenstance** wraps LLMs around LinkedIn/Gmail to surface who in your
  network knows a target contact.
- **Org change management:** Remy — "**Go slow to go far**," re-anchor in mission before
  rushing adoption; Gail — cross-functional working groups, not top-down mandates; shadow
  AI usage is common and better surfaced than suppressed; data security concerns somewhat
  overhyped (paid models with correct settings are generally secure; donor data can be
  de-identified).
- **Preserving institutional wisdom:** risk that AI smooths over the "productive friction"
  that builds junior staff expertise. Remy's fix — **"playbookification"**: use AI to
  interview tenured staff through their decision-making, capturing judgment and taste, not
  just process steps.

Takeaways:
- Ask "**is it good writing?**" not "is it AI-written?" — AI-detection focus is
  counterproductive.
- Natural language CRM interfaces are most exciting for empowering leadership, not just
  saving staff time.
- Tool integrations are improving fast; if something doesn't connect today, check again in
  6 months.

Action items from the session: explore **Happenstance** (free tier) and check CRM
integration; connect with Gail, Paul, and Remy on LinkedIn.
